NSF PlayerPrivacy Policy

Support

For help with NSF Player, email memtimen@gmail.com.

Include your device model, operating system version, app version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Please avoid including private information or music files.

Importing music

Music is not included. Import your own NSF or NSFE files, or supported 7z archives. M3U files can supply track titles and durations.

iCloud sync

Sync is off by default. Use the same Apple Account on your devices, enable iCloud sync in NSF Player Settings on each device, and allow time for your library to sync while connected to the internet. Turning sync off preserves existing local and iCloud data.

支持与反馈

如需帮助，请发邮件至 memtimen@gmail.com，并说明设备型号、系统版本、App 版本及问题复现步骤。请避免发送个人隐私信息或音乐文件。

导入音乐

App 不内置音乐。请导入你自己的 NSF／NSFE 文件或支持的 7z 压缩包，M3U 文件可提供曲名和时长。

iCloud 同步

同步默认关闭。在各设备上使用同一个 Apple 账户，并在每台设备的 NSF Player 设置中开启 iCloud 同步，联网后等待音乐库同步。关闭同步会保留已有的本机与 iCloud 数据。
